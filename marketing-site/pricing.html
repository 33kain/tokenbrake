Pricing questions

A few things people often ask about our plans.

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes. The Free plan supports up to 3 members and 5 active projects at no cost, and there's no time limit.

How does per-member pricing work?

Paid plans are billed for each member in your workspace. You can add or remove members at any time, and your bill adjusts accordingly.

What's the difference between Pro and Team?

Pro is built for a single growing team with up to 50 members. Team adds an admin console, roles and permissions, single sign-on, and more storage for organizations running many teams.

Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?

Yes. You can move between monthly and annual billing whenever you like. Annual billing is offered at a lower per-member rate.

Do you offer plans for larger organizations?

Yes. The Enterprise plan includes SAML SSO, SCIM provisioning, advanced security controls, and a dedicated success manager. Contact sales to build a plan for your organization.
