Features

Built to keep work moving

From planning to shipping, Nimbus gives your team a shared home for projects, documents, and decisions.

🗂️

Projects & boards

Plan work in boards, timelines, and lists. Drag tasks between stages and watch progress update for everyone at once.

📝

Docs & wikis

Write and organize documents alongside your projects, with rich formatting, mentions, and version history.

📊

Dashboards

Roll up progress across teams into custom views, so everyone sees the same status in real time.

🔔

Activity feed

A single feed of what changed keeps your team informed without extra meetings or long email threads.

🔗

Integrations

Connect messaging, calendars, and file storage to bring the updates you care about into one place.

🔒

Roles & access

Assign roles, invite guests, and share links with the exact level of access each person needs.

🔍

Search

Find any task, document, or comment across your workspace in seconds with fast, full-text search.

📱

Web & mobile

Work from the browser or on the go, with your projects and docs kept in sync across devices.

🧩

Templates

Start new projects from ready-made templates for launches, sprints, hiring, and more.
