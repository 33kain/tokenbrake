Our story

From a shared frustration to a shared workspace

We were a small team spending more time switching between tools than doing the work. So we built the workspace we wished we had, one place where projects, documents, and updates come together.

Today, teams in 130 countries use Nimbus to plan projects, write together, and stay in sync. We keep the product focused on the essentials, and we keep it calm.
